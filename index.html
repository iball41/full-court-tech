*Also worth knowing — specialized and regional tools
NacsportQwikCutDartfish HoopsalyticsCoachNowSportlyzer SkillCornerHudl InstatHudl Replay
These tools serve specific niches or regions. Nacsport and Dartfish are strong in European federations and Olympic programs. Hoopsalytics and QwikCut are affordable Hudl Assist alternatives for smaller programs. SkillCorner is emerging as a physical performance data layer for NCAA teams alongside KINEXON. Second Spectrum (now a Genius Sports company) is covered as a full tool card in Section 02 above — it remains active as the NBA's official team analytics and broadcast augmentation provider, and became the WNBA's official optical tracking provider in 2024.
Program maturity model

Build the right stack
for your level.

by Imani Dunbar

Five illustrative tiers from grassroots to NBA — showing which tools to adopt, in what order, and what capability each unlocks. Every program's needs are different; use this as a starting framework, not a prescription.

ℹ
Every program's needs, priorities, and existing infrastructure will differ significantly. Sample models based on general program archetypes. Budget ranges are illustrative estimates.
How to use this model
Find your current level, then look one tier up to understand which tools and integrations to prioritize next. Each level builds on the previous — tools are rarely replaced outright, they're layered on top of what already exists in the program.

01
Grassroots
Youth & recreational programs
Building habits, not systems. The goal is engagement and basic skill feedback.
Illustrative budget
Free – $500/yr
Sample stack
HomeCourt
Full individual loop — capture, analyze, output
SportsVisio (free tier)
Basic team stat tracking from uploaded video
What this unlocks
Shot tracking and skill metrics without any equipment investment
Basic game stats without manual scorekeeping burden on staff
Shareable highlight clips for players, parents, and social media
→
At this level, the entire stack lives on a phone. HomeCourt gives young players a feedback loop they can act on independently — which is the single most important capability a developing athlete can have before entering an institutional program.
02
Developing
High school varsity programs
First institutional tools. Video and data begin informing coaching decisions.
Illustrative budget
$500 – $5k/yr
Sample stack

Noah Basketball

What this unlocks
HD game film at every venue without a dedicated cameraman
35+ data points per game without a video coordinator on staff
Objective shot mechanics feedback players can act on in practice
Digital plays delivered to players' phones instead of paper printouts
→
The Focus Flex → Hudl Assist pipeline is the single best investment at this level. The camera films, Assist tags, and you coach. No additional staff needed at the capture or tagging layers.

At this level, Synergy becomes the hub everything else orbits. The video coordinator lives in Sportscode, the analytics staff works in Synergy, the sports science staff runs Catapult or KINEXON, and the athletic trainer monitors WHOOP or Oura — and FastModel is where all three outputs converge into a game plan the players actually see.

At the NBA level, teams don't just use these tools — they build on them. Hawk-Eye's pose data and Synergy's play-type database become raw material for proprietary models built by in-house data scientists. The tools listed here are the foundation; what sits on top is each franchise's competitive secret.
